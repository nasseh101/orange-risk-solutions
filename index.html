About the company

Officially registered on 1st March 2022 by PACRA and licensed by the Pensions and Insurance Authority (PIA) on 1st January 2023, the organization is a fully subscribed member of the Insurance Brokers Association of Zambia (IBAZ).

The organisation consists of seasoned professionals dedicated to providing quality and tailored services to meet the various needs of large corporates, small and medium enterprises (SMEs), individuals and non-governmental organisations.
